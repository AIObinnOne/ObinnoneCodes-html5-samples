Five Lessons From a Year of Balcony Gardening

Posted on September 12, 2026 by Lena Ortiz

1. Sunlight Beats Everything

I tracked the light for a week before planting. Six hours of direct sun turned out to be a luxury only one corner enjoyed.

2. Bigger Pots, Fewer Worries

Small containers dry out by noon in July. Switching to deep pots halved my watering.

3. Herbs Are Forgiving

Basil, mint, and chives survived every mistake I made. Start with them.

4. Wind Is the Hidden Enemy

Tall plants snapped twice before I added stakes and a simple mesh screen.

5. Keep a Notebook

Dates, weather, and harvests on paper made next year's plan easy.
